Staff only see, update and get history and stats for the shops ticked for them. They never see the warehouses. Admins see every storeroom and can manage users and settings. Give the person their username and starting password; they can change it under Account.

Ticking or unticking a shop saves straight away and takes effect the next time the person loads a page. Revoking signs the person out on every device straight away. Their past updates stay in the history.

Low-stock alert

This applies to every tyre in every storeroom. To set a different level for one tyre, add a column named Min to the stock sheet and type the level on that tyre's row. A Min of 0 turns alerts off for that tyre (useful for sizes you've stopped selling).
